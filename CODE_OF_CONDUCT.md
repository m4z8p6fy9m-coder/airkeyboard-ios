# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome differing experience levels, give specific constructive feedback and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, sexualized attention, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and limit participation, using proportionate responses and protecting reporter privacy. Report conduct issues privately through GitHub's available reporting tools or an explicitly published maintainer contact. No private email/contact is invented in this initial repository. Once maintainers establish a reporting contact, document it here.
